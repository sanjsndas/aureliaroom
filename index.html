Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to start an interior design project?
Begin with a clear brief: how you want to feel in the space, functional requirements, and a realistic budget. Collect visual references, but focus on principles like light, flow, and proportion before choosing finishes.
How do I make a small room feel larger?
Maximize natural light, use a consistent floor material, choose furniture with legs (to show floor space), and employ large mirrors. Avoid heavy curtains and dark corners.
Are sustainable materials worth the investment?
Yes. Responsibly sourced timber, low-VOC finishes, and recycled content materials improve indoor air quality and durability. They often age better and reduce long-term maintenance.
How important is lighting in a renovation?
Lighting is transformative. Plan ambient, task, and accent layers early — before electrical rough-in. Dimmers and warm color temperatures (2700K–3000K) create flexibility.
