We write about the quiet details that make a space feel like home.

Aurelia Room Theory is an independent editorial publication exploring interior design, architecture, materials, lighting, and the art of modern living. Founded in 2019, we publish thoughtful essays, guides, and project studies for design professionals and enthusiasts alike.

Our Mission

We believe good design is not about trends or expensive objects — it is about the way light falls on a wall, the proportion of a doorway, the tactile quality of a hand-finished surface. Aurelia Room Theory exists to slow down the conversation around interiors and architecture, focusing on principles that endure rather than fashions that fade.

Every article we publish is original, thoroughly researched, and free from sponsored influence. We are reader-supported and committed to clarity, depth, and usefulness. Whether you are an architect, a designer, or simply someone who cares deeply about the spaces you inhabit, we hope you find something here that helps you see your surroundings with fresh eyes.

Editorial Independence

We do not accept sponsored posts, paid product placements, or affiliate arrangements that compromise our editorial voice. Our recommendations are based on research, experience, and a genuine belief in the value of thoughtful design.

We are supported by our readers through newsletter subscriptions and occasional partnerships that align with our values. This model allows us to remain curious, critical, and independent.

Mira Voss

Founding Editor

Mira is an interior architect and writer exploring the intersection of material culture and domestic space. She has over fifteen years of experience in residential and adaptive-reuse projects across Europe.
