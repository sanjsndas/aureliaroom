Before you write

Quick answers to common questions about working with us.

Do you accept guest posts or sponsored content?
We rarely accept guest posts, and only when they align closely with our editorial voice. Sponsored content is clearly labelled and must be relevant to our audience. Please email partners@aureliaroomtheory.com with details.
Can I republish an article from Aurelia Room Theory?
You may quote short excerpts with clear attribution and a link back. Full republishing requires written permission. Contact editors@aureliaroomtheory.com.
I spotted a factual error. How do I report it?
We appreciate corrections. Please use the contact form and select “Correction / feedback”, including the article title and the specific detail. We’ll review and update promptly.
Do you offer design consultations?
Aurelia Room Theory is an editorial publication, not a design studio. We don’t take on client projects, but we’re happy to recommend practices we trust.
